# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Data - Exploratory Data Analysis
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb316409-3578-442f-b8a0-dda9c02c1ac3)

This repository contains an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of chocolate sales data. The analysis focuses on cleaning, transforming, and visualizing the data to uncover insights such as total sales by country, top-performing salespeople, and product-level performance.

## 📌 Objectives
- Clean and preprocess the dataset
- Explore key performance indicators:
- Total sales by country
- Top 10 salespeople by total sales
- Average revenue per box by product
- Total boxes shipped by country
-Sales amount distribution by product
- Sales by country and product
- Visualize findings using plots and charts

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries
- Python (developed in Visual Studio Code)
- pandas – data manipulation
- numpy – numerical operations
- seaborn – statistical visualization
- matplotlib – plotting and charting

## 📊 Key Analyses
✅ Total Sales by Country
Analyzed total revenue generated per country to identify the most profitable regions.

✅ Top 10 Salespeople by Total Sales
Identified the top performers in the sales team based on total revenue generated.

✅ Average Revenue per Box by Product
Calculated and visualized the average earnings per box for each product type.

✅ Total Boxes Shipped by Country
Analyzed logistics performance by country in terms of product volume.

✅ Sales Amount Distribution by Product
Visualized the variation in sales amounts across different product types.

✅ Sales by Country and Product
Used heatmaps and grouped bar charts to understand product preferences by region.

## 📈 Results & Insights
- Certain countries generate significantly higher revenue, suggesting strong market potential.
- Top salespeople contribute disproportionately to total revenue.
- Some products have much higher average revenue per box—indicating premium positioning.
- Sales distributions show potential product bundling or pricing optimization opportunities.
